DECISIONES DE PRODUCTO

Construye solo lo que el usuario necesita.

The demo deliberately avoids pretending to be an ERP. It shows a small product surface where architecture decisions are visible through actual interactions.

Propietario

Can switch businesses, manage data and access reports.

Manager-only actions can be hidden from other roles.

Tenant context

Switching businesses changes the data scope without changing identity.

No cross-tenant data is shown in the UI.
